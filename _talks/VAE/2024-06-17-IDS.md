One of my favourite conferences during my PhD! The Insurance Data Science Conference brings together two parallel perspectives, with an academic track and an industry track, which made it especially interesting to meet and exchange with people working in insurance outside academia. I really enjoyed the mix of research and industry discussions, and Stockholm itself was definitely a highlight — I may have slightly fallen for the Stockholm archipelago! I don't have any photos from the conference itself, but I have plenty from the weekend kayaking trip around the archipelago afterwards.

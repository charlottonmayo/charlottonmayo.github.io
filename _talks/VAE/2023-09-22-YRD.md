About a year into my PhD, I presented my second PhD project for the first time at the Young Researcher Day organised by my department at UCLouvain. The event is a great opportunity to share our research with fellow colleagues in a more friendly atmosphere. I have particularly good memories of this presentation — it was probably the first time I really felt confident presenting my work. I introduced my new project on generating synthetic insurance data, which is also where my interest in Bayesian statistics really started to grow.

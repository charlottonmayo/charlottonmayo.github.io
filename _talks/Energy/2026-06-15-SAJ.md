Back to Stockholm, and back to my first research project on fairness — this time presenting it after its publication in the *North American Actuarial Journal*. This was probably my best presentation to date. It was also my first conference with my PhD supervisor, Prof. Donatien Hainaut!

The timing made it even more special: this was just two weeks after my private PhD defence on 28 May, so it felt a little like the beginning of the next chapter. And of course, no trip to Stockholm would be complete without some time in the archipelago — got to experience Midsommar in a cabin afterwards.

Back to the Young Researcher Day at UCLouvain, almost exactly two years after presenting my second PhD project there for the first time. This time, I presented my final PhD project: FairGGPR, a multi-criteria fair Gaussian regressor for insurance pricing. It was also the first time I introduced its unofficial nickname, the Pesh Mel' Bush model.

It was a nice full-circle moment to come back to the YRD with the project that would eventually become the last piece of my PhD research, and to see how much things had happily changed since my first presentation there two years earlier.

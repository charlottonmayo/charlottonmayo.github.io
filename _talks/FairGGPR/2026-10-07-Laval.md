A few days in Québec City 🍁 I had the chance to present my research on the FairGGPR and take part in the panel discussion *“Methodological Challenges of Fairness in Insurance”*.

The panel was definitely a whole new opportunity to learn. Having representatives from Ontario’s insurance regulator and experts in ethics and discrimination join the discussions made the whole experience even more insightful.

Great people, lots of laughs, beautiful Old Québec, and maple pecan popcorn.

My first seminar! I was invited by Peter Hieber — after failing to keep my promise to stop by Lausanne during my summer trek through France, Italy, and Switzerland — to present my work on fairness in insurance pricing at the Actuarial Seminar at the University of Lausanne. The project was still in its early stages at the time, so it was also a fun opportunity to share some ideas that were very much still taking shape. It was a nice change from conferences and workshops, with a bit more time to dive into the research and discuss it properly.

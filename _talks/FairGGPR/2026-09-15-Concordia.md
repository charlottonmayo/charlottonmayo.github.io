First presentation of my postdoc! After defending my PhD on 20 August and arriving in Montréal two weeks later, I gave my first one-hour research seminar at Concordia University as part of the CANSSI Quebec Postdoc Day. I had the chance to meet other postdoctoral researchers along the way. It was also the first time I had the opportunity to properly present the Bayesian mathematics behind FairGGPR, rather than having to compress the methodology into a few slides and move on to the results.

The whole UQÀM team also came to attend my talk. I've always valued this kind of thing in research groups — showing up for each other — and it made me especially happy to find that spirit at UQÀM.

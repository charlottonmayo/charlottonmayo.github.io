First presentation of my postdoc! After defending my PhD on 20 August and arriving in Montréal two weeks later, I gave my first one-hour research seminar at Concordia University as part of the CANSSI Quebec Postdoc Day. I had the chance to meet other postdoctoral researchers along the way. 

I particularly enjoy this kind of seminar format. Having a full hour gives you enough time to go beyond simply presenting the results: you can properly explain the motivation behind the research, take the time to develop the ideas, and actually delve into the mathematics (without losing everyone right away). It was the first time I had the opportunity to properly present the Bayesian mathematics behind FairGGPR, rather than having to compress the methodology into a few slides and move on to the results.

The whole UQÀM team also came to attend my talk, which was incredibly kind and made the first seminar of my postdoc even more special.

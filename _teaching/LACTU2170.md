### Main topics

- Financial mathematics and interest-rate modelling
- Option pricing
- Black–Scholes models
- Stochastic calculus
- Numerical methods for actuarial valuation
- Applications to actuarial liabilities

### My role

My role focused on preparing and teaching exercise sessions, guiding students through problem-solving, answering questions, and helping bridge the gap between the theoretical material covered in lectures and its practical application.

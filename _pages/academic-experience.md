  </h2>

  <div class="education-keywords">
    non-life insurance · Bayesian modelling · machine learning · fairness · uncertainty
  </div>

  <p class="education-preview">
    PhD research at ISBA (UCLouvain) in actuarial science under the supervision of
    <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/donatienhainaut/home">
  <strong>Prof. Donatien Hainaut</strong></a>, focusing on statistical and
    machine learning methods for non-life insurance. My PhD research was funded through the Excellence of Science (EoS) programme, a joint research funding initiative of the F.R.S.-FNRS (Fédération Wallonie-Bruxelles) and the FWO (Flanders Research Foundation).
  </p>

  <details>
    <summary>

    <div class="education-expanded">

      <p>
        My research investigated how modern statistical and machine learning
        methods can be used in non-life insurance while maintaining
        interpretability, actuarial consistency, and appropriate treatment
        of predictive uncertainty.
      </p>

      <p>Skills developed include:</p>

      <ul>
        <li>
          <strong>Statistical and actuarial modelling:</strong> developing
          models for insurance claim frequency and risk prediction.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Bayesian modelling:</strong> working with probabilistic
          models and methods for quantifying epistemic and predictive uncertainty.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Machine learning:</strong> implementing, analysing, and
          comparing machine learning models with classical actuarial approaches.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Fairness and responsible modelling:</strong> investigating
          how fairness considerations can be incorporated into predictive
          insurance models and how they affect model behaviour and uncertainty.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Model interpretability:</strong> analysing and communicating
          model predictions and the influence of individual risk factors.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Computational research:</strong> implementing statistical
          and machine learning methodologies and conducting empirical studies
          using real-world and simulated data.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Academic writing:</strong> producing research papers,
          presenting results at conferences and seminars, and communicating
          technical findings to academic audiences.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Research collaboration:</strong> working with researchers
          across statistics, machine learning, and actuarial science.
        </li>
      </ul>

    </div>

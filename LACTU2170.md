Teaching assistant for LACTU2170 'Valorisation financière des engagements actuariels' during the 2024--2025 and 2025--2026 academic years.
======
Conducted the exercise and practical sessions for Master's students in actuarial science and related programmes. Topics included financial mathematics, interest-rate modelling, option pricing, Black--Scholes models, stochastic calculus, and numerical methods for actuarial valuation.

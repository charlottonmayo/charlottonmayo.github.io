This article explores the application of Variational AutoEncoders to heterogeneous insurance portfolios containing mixed continuous and discrete attributes. The proposed architecture addresses dimensionality reduction and synthetic data generation while using a quantile transformation for continuous variables and a reconstruction loss adapted to mixed-type insurance data.

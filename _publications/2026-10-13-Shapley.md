The paper develops a Wasserstein-based framework for auditing distributional fairness across multiple sensitive attributes, capturing intersectional disparities that marginal audits may overlook. It introduces an exact decomposition of joint distributional disparities through conditional quantile processes and uses Shapley values to allocate these disparities across sensitive attributes in an order-independent manner, accounting for interactions, suppression, and redundancy.
